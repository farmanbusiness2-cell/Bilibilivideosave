Complete Guide — Bilibili Video Download

This section is for users who are downloading from Bilibili for the first time. We explain every step in detail below.

Step 1: How to Find the Bilibili Video URL

First, you need the URL of the video you want to download. There are two ways:

  • On desktop: Open the Bilibili video in your browser → copy the URL from the address bar (e.g., https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1xx411c7mD)
  • On mobile app: Open the video → tap the "Share" icon on the right → select "Copy Link"

Step 2: Paste the URL

Now paste the URL into the BiliSave downloader box. You can also use b23.tv short links — we automatically handle the redirect.

Step 3: Select the Quality

Choose your desired quality from the dropdown:

  • 1080p (Full HD): Best quality, large file size (approx. 200-500 MB per 10 min)
  • 720p (HD): Balanced quality and size (approx. 100-250 MB per 10 min)
  • 480p (SD): Smallest size, best for mobile (approx. 50-100 MB per 10 min)

Step 4: Click the Download Button

Download links take 5-15 seconds to generate. If the video is private or region-locked, you will see an error message.

Step 5: Save the File

After clicking the download button, the video will be saved to your device. Check your default download folder.

Troubleshooting — Common Errors & Solutions

Sometimes errors can occur during the download process. Here are the common errors and their solutions:

❌ Error: "Invalid URL"

Cause: The URL is in the wrong format or is from another site. Solution: Make sure the URL contains bilibili.com/video/BV... or b23.tv/... Only the BV ID part will also work.

❌ Error: "Video not found"

Cause: The video was deleted, is private, or is region-locked. Solution: Check on Bilibili whether the video is still accessible.

❌ Error: "Service unavailable"

Cause: Cobalt.tools is temporarily busy or down. Solution: Wait 30-60 seconds and retry. Or go directly to cobalt.tools and paste the URL there.

❌ Download button doesn't work

Cause: Bilibili CDN checks the Referer header. Solution: Right-click the link → select "Save link as...". Or open the link in a new tab.

❌ 1080p option not showing

Cause: The original video was not uploaded in 1080p, or 1080p is only available to premium users. Solution: Try 720p. Some videos on Bilibili are only uploaded up to 720p.
